Model and assumptions

ΔG(r) = 4πr²γ + (4/3)πr³ΔGv
ΔGv(T) ≈ −Hv(Tm − T)/Tm   (Turnbull approximation)
r* = 2γ/|ΔGv|,    ΔG* = 16πγ³/(3|ΔGv|²)

Illustrative aluminum-like parameters: melting temperature Tm = 933 K; latent heat per unit volume Hv = 1.07 × 10⁹ J/m³. Hv is positive; ΔGv = Gsolid − Gliquid is negative below Tm.

Interpretation: Below r*, increasing the radius raises ΔG, so shrinking is favored. Above r*, increasing the radius lowers ΔG, so growth is favored. The critical nucleus is an unstable maximum, not a stable particle size.
